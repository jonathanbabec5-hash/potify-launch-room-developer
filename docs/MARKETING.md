# Beneath the Surface · Launch Room marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Developers and first-time ecosystem visitors. **Offer.** Give projects a shared doorway into POTIFY's public work.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show the route from the Launch Room to Plant Doctor, the public record and Reclaim. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Choose a project from the launch directory. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 10 qualified handoffs. |

### Sales test

Measure qualified product handoffs. Attribute a subsequent shop order only if the storefront can reconcile it; do not price traffic as a sale. Connect a directory referral to a fulfilled product order, with an opt-in referral label where available.

### Measurement and decision

Use `launch-room-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** The export excludes operational material and participant-level allocation rows from the original homepage. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
